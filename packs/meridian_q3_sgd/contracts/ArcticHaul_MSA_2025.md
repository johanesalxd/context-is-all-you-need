# Cold-Chain Carrier Master Services Agreement — ArcticHaul (MSA-ARCTIC-2025)

- **Parties:** Meridian Commerce Pte. Ltd. ("Shipper") and ArcticHaul Cold Logistics Pte. Ltd. ("Carrier")
- **Effective Term:** 2025-01-01 through 2027-12-31
- **Covered Lanes:** Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia cold-chain linehaul & hub transfers
- **Contract ID:** `MSA-ARCTIC-2025`

## Section 4 · Baseline Thermal Excursion Service Level Agreement (SLA)

1. **Standard Thermal Threshold:** Carrier shall maintain continuous cold-chain cabin telemetry between `+0.5°C` and `+4.0°C` for chilled consignments.
2. **Legacy Breach Trigger:** A compensable SLA excursion occurs when calibrated logger telemetry records peak cabin temperature strictly above **`4.0°C`** for a continuous duration of **`90` minutes or more** (`temp_peak_celsius > 4.0 AND excursion_duration_min >= 90`).
3. **Baseline Service Credit:** Upon verified legacy breach, Shipper is entitled to a **`5%` (`0.05`)** freight invoice credit against the affected shipment's commercial invoice (`shipment_invoice_sgd`), settled quarterly.
4. **Precedence:** Executed SKU-specific written amendments override Section 4 baseline thresholds and credit rates from their stated effective date.
