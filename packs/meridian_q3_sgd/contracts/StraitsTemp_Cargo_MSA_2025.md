# Cold-Chain Carrier Master Services Agreement — StraitsTemp Cargo (MSA-STRAITS-2025)

- **Parties:** Meridian Commerce Pte. Ltd. ("Shipper") and StraitsTemp Cargo Sdn. Bhd. ("Carrier")
- **Effective Term:** 2025-04-15 through 2028-04-14
- **Contract ID:** `MSA-STRAITS-2025`

## Section 4 · Thermal Excursion Service Level Agreement (SLA)

1. **Covered Classes:** `CHILLED_FOOD` and `FROZEN_PHARMA` regional transfer lanes.
2. **Breach Trigger:** Compensable excursion occurs when calibrated telemetry exceeds **`4.0°C`** for **`90` continuous minutes or more** (`temp_peak_celsius > 4.0 AND excursion_duration_min >= 90`).
3. **Service Credit:** **`5%` (`0.05`)** of `shipment_invoice_sgd` per qualifying breach. No executed amendment overlays apply to Q3 2026 shipments.
