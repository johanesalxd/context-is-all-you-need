# Cold-Chain Carrier Master Services Agreement — SiamFrost Logistics (MSA-SIAM-2025)

- **Parties:** Meridian Commerce Pte. Ltd. ("Shipper") and SiamFrost Logistics Co., Ltd. ("Carrier")
- **Effective Term:** 2025-03-01 through 2028-02-28
- **Contract ID:** `MSA-SIAM-2025`

## Section 4 · Thermal Excursion Service Level Agreement (SLA)

1. **Covered Classes:** `CHILLED_FOOD` and `AMBIENT_STD` cross-border Thailand–Malaysia–Singapore lanes.
2. **Breach Trigger:** Compensable excursion occurs when calibrated telemetry exceeds **`4.0°C`** for **`90` continuous minutes or more** (`temp_peak_celsius > 4.0 AND excursion_duration_min >= 90`).
3. **Service Credit:** **`5%` (`0.05`)** of `shipment_invoice_sgd` per qualifying breach, with no SKU-specific amendment in effect for Q3 2026.
